# Behavior by Design — Format Bible

Full detail: [docs/PLAYBOOK.md](../../docs/PLAYBOOK.md), Channel 2.
Machine-readable rules: [channel.config.json](channel.config.json).

## Positioning

Behavior by Design explains the hidden wiring behind what people do, and
the systems built around that wiring.

**Unifying thesis: your reaction may be human. The setup may be
intentional.**

## The editorial split (binding)

- **Family A, Built into you:** psychology, evolution, attention, memory,
  social behaviour, perception.
- **Family B, Built around you:** retail design, pricing, software,
  interfaces, offices, airports, subscriptions, institutions.

Long-term balance ~50/50. The split blocks the four failure modes: generic
psychology facts, dark-pattern outrage, business-breakdown clone,
conspiracy channel. It is also a revenue decision — Family B carries the
higher RPM.

## Tone (binding)

Calm, mechanical, curious, dry, non-moralising. No outrage narrator. No
"companies are evil" simplification. No fake certainty about contested
psychology — contested findings are flagged as contested **on screen**.

## The red rule

Red marks only: the behavioural trigger, the design choice, the moment of
manipulation, the hidden incentive, or the mechanism itself. Never
decorative. Within three episodes viewers read red as "this is the point"
before the narrator says it. Cheapest brand asset in the network — protect
it.

## The style string is non-negotiable

Every image prompt reuses the exact character construction and style
language verbatim from `channel.config.json`, never a paraphrase. Prompt
drift off that string is what caused the multipotentiality frames to fail,
and it is the single cheapest production failure to prevent. The
scene-breakdown agent injects it programmatically.

## Production notes specific to this channel

- One core mechanism per episode. One practical reframe or tell at the end.
- Separate established findings from speculation explicitly in the script
  file.
- Fact gate is automated on this channel (no mandatory human sign-off),
  but the source-URL requirement still holds.
- Shorts come from the recognition beat and the red-mark beat.
- Test curiosity-led titles against second-person titles and record which
  family wins.
- From day 46: visible study citations on screen — the cheapest available
  differentiator in this niche.
