Replaced because the produced Episode 1 frames are isometric, not hand-drawn marker.

Hand-drawn financial investigation map on white paper with subtle fibre texture. Black marker linework, slightly imperfect, confident single-weight strokes. Flat illustration, no shading, no gradients. A single accent colour: green (#1B7F4D) marking recoverable money only; grey marking money that is gone; all other elements black on white. Frame objects drawn as simple marker sketches: claim forms, letters, payment trails, timelines, document stacks, portal screens, eligibility trees, money moving between institutions. Exactly one highlighted amount or one missing step per frame, never two. Clean negative space, documentary tone, no cartoon exaggeration, no photorealism, no 3D.
