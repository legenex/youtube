Tax forfeiture surplus funds · unclaimed balances

Parcels sold at tax forfeiture sale for which proceeds remain on deposit with this office in excess of the tax debt satisfied. Former owners of record, or their heirs, may claim the surplus. There is no charge to file a claim.
You do not need to pay anyone to recover these funds. This office does not endorse, employ, or share records with any recovery, locator, or finder service. A claim filed directly costs nothing.
