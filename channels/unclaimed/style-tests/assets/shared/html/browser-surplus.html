Unclaimed surplus funds

Parcels sold at tax forfeiture sale for which proceeds remain on deposit in excess of the tax debt satisfied. Former owners of record, or their heirs, may file a claim. There is no fee to file a claim.
You do not need to pay anyone to recover these funds. This office does not endorse or share records with any recovery, locator, or finder service.
