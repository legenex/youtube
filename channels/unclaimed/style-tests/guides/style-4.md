# Style 4 · High Speed Cut

## Look in one sentence
Something changes every half second in a serious register, so the speed itself
becomes the argument that this is urgent.

## Palette
| Hex | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `#0D0D0D` | Ground and the flash colour for neutral beats. |
| `#E8E8E8` | Mono type. Every statement. |
| `#3AAE5C` | Money, and the emerald two frame flash that punctuates a figure. |
| `#C0392B` | The county. One red, used for the actor who took the money, and for one flash. |

Footage grade is lighter than style 2: saturation 0.34, contrast 1.28. The
images need to be readable in half a second, so they cannot be crushed.

## Typography
Helvetica Neue Black Condensed, upper case, centred. Statements 52 to 98 px on
a 720 px frame. Payoff words 120 to 200 px. One line per shot, never two: at
this speed a second line cannot be read. Letter spacing 0.01 to 0.14 em. Text
sits at 250 to 330 px from the top, so it never fights the lower edge.

## Shot grammar
A shot is one image, one zoom punch of 8 to 22 percent, and at most one line of
type. Every cut is hard. Two frame flashes of solid colour sit between beats
that need separation, emerald after a figure, red after the county, ink after a
statement. Documents appear as inverted crops so they cut cleanly against
photographic frames. The grammar is relentless but not random: images alternate
photographic, document, photographic, so the eye keeps re-anchoring.

## Rhythm spec
About 159 shots per minute. Maximum static hold 1 second; measured 0.70.
Average interval 0.38 s. There are no pauses. The only concession is the final
line, which gets two shots instead of one so the sign-off lands rather than
flies past.

## Asset recipe
About 49 percent generated stills, 9 percent generated clips, 21 percent
document crops, 21 percent solid colour flashes. The flashes and documents cost
nothing, which is why this style is far cheaper than its shot count suggests.

## Cost per 10 minute episode
This is the expensive one. At 159 shots per minute a 10 minute episode needs
roughly 1,600 distinct visual beats. Reuse is mandatory: budget about 60 stills
and 12 clips, then cut each asset 8 to 12 ways with different crops, zooms and
type. Generation calls: about 72. Stock clips: 40 or more if available.
Editing: about 14 hours.

## Three rules that must never be broken
1. No jokes, no memes, no irony. The pacing is borrowed from tech explainers,
   the register is not.
2. One line of type per shot.
3. No flash longer than two frames, and never a flash on the sign-off.

## What this style is bad at
Anything that needs to be read or understood. It cannot carry a document, a
process, a caveat or a number the viewer has to check. It also cannot breathe,
so it is exhausting at length and it fights the calm plain voice this channel
uses. Best as a 20 second opening, not a whole episode.
