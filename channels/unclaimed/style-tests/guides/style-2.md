# Style 2 · Kinetic Type Over Footage

## Look in one sentence
Bold condensed type slams onto the beat while heavily graded footage runs
underneath as a texture, never as the subject.

## Palette
| Hex | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `#0D0D0D` | Ground. The grade crushes all footage toward this. |
| `#F5F0E3` | Body type. Every statement line. |
| `#3AAE5C` | Money and the sign-off payoff. Emerald means a dollar figure or the promise. |
| `#D8B26A` | Warm tan for figures that are not the payoff, so 15,000 and 40,000 read differently from 25,000. |
| `#C0392B` | The county. Used once, as a block behind "Hennepin County". |

Footage is graded to saturation 0.14, contrast 1.22, brightness minus 0.22. It
should be barely legible as a place.

## Typography
Impact throughout, upper case, no exceptions. Statement lines 56 to 66 px on a
720 px frame. Payoff words 96 to 138 px, about one fifth of frame height.
Figures 168 to 190 px, the largest thing that ever appears. Type is centred or
flush left at 70 px, never flush right. Letter spacing 0.01 to 0.08 em, opened
up as size drops. Every line carries a soft black shadow so it holds over any
footage.

## Shot grammar
A shot is one graded plate with one or two type lines. Lines arrive staggered,
about 0.22 s apart, each dropping in over three frames from 20 px above its
rest position, then locking hard. Colour blocks wipe across in 0.20 s on a new
statement. Figures resolve out of redaction blocks: the number arrives as
`$██,███`, then digits fill left to right, then it locks. That reveal invents
no intermediate figure, which matters on this channel. Cuts are hard on the
voice.

## Rhythm spec
About 41 shots per minute. Maximum static hold 2.5 seconds; measured 1.90.
Pauses sit on the two shortest sentences, "Not the debt" and "All of it", which
get their own plates so the silence is visible.

## Asset recipe
About 82 percent generated stills, 18 percent generated clips, zero document,
zero screen recording. Any stock library would substitute directly for the
generated footage, since the footage only needs to read as a dark texture.

## Cost per 10 minute episode
Generation calls: 0 if stock is available, since this style does not need
specific imagery. Otherwise about 20 stills and 6 clips. Stock clips: 20 to 30.
Editing: about 6 hours, most of it timing type to the voice track.

## Three rules that must never be broken
1. Type is the subject. If the footage is competing, grade it down further.
2. Emerald only for money and the sign-off. Tan for supporting figures.
3. Never render a figure that is not in the script, not even for a frame of a
   count up. Use the redaction reveal instead.

## What this style is bad at
Nuance and sequence. It states, it does not explain, so it cannot carry a
process, a chronology or a caveat. It also gets tiring: past about 90 seconds
the slams stop registering.
