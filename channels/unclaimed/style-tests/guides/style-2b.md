# Style 2B · Kinetic Type, Two Modes

**Matrix (slam):** generated footage / near black / type / fast slam.
**Matrix (sustain):** generated footage / near black / type over legible plate /
steady procedural.

## Look in one sentence
Style 2's type with two gears: it slams, then drops into a register where the
footage becomes the subject, and keeps trading.

## Palette
| Hex | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `#0D0D0D` | Ground. Slam crushes footage toward this. |
| `#F5F0E3` | Body type, both modes. |
| `#3AAE5C` | Money and the sign-off. In sustain, one thing only: a live figure in the rail, or one underline under the field being pointed at. |
| `#D8B26A` | Supporting figures, so a second number reads differently. |

Slam grade: saturation 0.14, contrast 1.22, brightness minus 0.22. Sustain:
0.55, 1.05, minus 0.05, because there you are meant to read the plate.

## Typography
Impact, upper case. Slam as Style 2 specifies: statements 56 to 66 px, payoffs
96 to 138 px, figures 168 to 190 px. Sustain drops to 32 to 40 px in a lower
third or left rail, sentence case allowed for a URL.

## Shot grammar
**Slam.** Exactly Style 2. Lines stagger 0.22 s apart, drop from 20 px above,
lock hard. Figures resolve out of redaction blocks. Carries statements, figures
and turns. About 25 percent.

**Sustain.** Type sits in the rail while the plate plays. Screen recordings and
documents live here. Carries process, sequence, caveats and instructions. About
75 percent.

**Alternation rule.** Never more than 20 seconds of slam without dropping to
sustain, never more than 40 seconds of sustain without a slam. The change is a
hard cut, never a fade, and the grade shifts on that cut. This is what lets the
style run 10 minutes.

**A resource name** is a sustain moment: the URL holds in the rail while the
real page plays full frame. Never slam a URL; a viewer copying it down needs it
still.

**The one common mistake** is a slam: it is a statement, so it takes the centre,
upper case, full size, on a crushed plate.

## Rhythm spec
Slam about 41 shots per minute, maximum hold 2.5 s. Sustain 15 to 20, cuts of 3
to 4 s.

## Asset recipe
Slam: generated or stock footage as texture. Sustain: screen recordings and
document facsimiles, which cost nothing.

## Cost per 10 minute episode
About 8 stills, 3 clips, 6 to 10 screen recordings, 7 hours edit.

## Three rules that must never be broken
1. Obey the alternation rule. One gear is what broke Style 2.
2. Mode changes are hard cuts, and the grade moves on the cut.
3. Never render a figure not in the script. Use the redaction reveal.

## What this style is bad at
Warmth and place. The palette stays cold in both modes, so it cannot carry a
human story. Sustain needs real screen recordings; without them it collapses
back into Style 2.
