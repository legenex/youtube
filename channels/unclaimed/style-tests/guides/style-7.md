# Style 7 · Tabletop Overhead

**Matrix:** photographed objects / warm light / physical object / slow reveal.

## Look in one sentence
A camera above a desk in warm daylight, where every figure arrives as a printed
sheet slid into frame and something is always moving.

## Palette
| Hex | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `#F4EDDF` | Paper stock. Every sheet is this, never white. |
| `#2A2118` | Printed type. Warm black, never neutral black. |
| `#8A7A5E` | The mono kicker above a figure, saying what it is. |
| `#9A3F30` | The stamp. One per sheet at most, and only on a status. |

Plates are graded to contrast 1.04, brightness plus 0.10, saturation 1.16, gamma
1.18, warmed on red, which lifts a tungsten plate toward window light.

## Typography
Public Sans Bold for a figure, Source Serif for a sentence, IBM Plex Mono for the
kicker. All of it is printed, so it carries a soft shadow and sits on stock. If
type is on screen, it is on paper.

## Shot grammar
A shot is one plate plus one sheet. The sheet travels in from an edge over 0.36
to 0.42 s, linearly, and stops. It never fades, never scales and never rotates
on entry. Plate identity alternates on every cut, because at this pace two
consecutive shots of the same surface do not read as a cut at all.

## Rhythm spec
About 31 shots per minute. Maximum static hold 2.5 s; measured 2.10. The slowest
style in the set, and the only one that can hold a beat without dying.

## Asset recipe
Photographic plates plus HTML printed sheets. The sheets cost nothing. The
plates are the only real spend in the style.

## Cost per 10 minute episode
About 10 to 14 overhead stills and 3 clips, or a half day of real photography,
which is cheaper and better. Editing about 6 hours.

## Three rules that must never be broken
1. Figures arrive on paper. A number that appears as a screen graphic is a bug.
2. Something enters or leaves every shot. A still tabletop is a dead frame.
3. No printed sheet may carry a figure, name or date the script does not state.

## What this style is bad at
Scale and abstraction. It cannot show a system, a country or a total, only one
desk. Pace is its other limit: it cannot be cut fast without looking frantic.
