# Style 5 · Case File

## Look in one sentence
A manila folder opens on a dark desk under one warm side light, and the evidence
accumulates: documents taped down, photographs at slight angles, typewriter
labels stamped on, red thread connecting two facts.

## Palette
| Hex | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `#17110A` to `#0B0806` | The desk. A warm near black, lit from upper left only. |
| `#CFA863` | The folder. Manila, the one large warm mass in frame. |
| `#EFE9DA` | Paper. Documents and typewriter labels. |
| `#3AAE5C` | Emerald label, used once, for the surplus that was kept. |
| `#8D2013` | Red thread, pins and the vote stamp. Connection and finding. |

## Typography
Courier New Bold throughout, because everything is a typed label. Labels 30 px,
figure labels 54 px on a 720 px frame, upper case, letter spaced 0.14 to
0.20 em, on paper plates with a hard drop shadow. Labels are rotated 1 to 3
degrees. Nothing is ever perfectly straight in this style.

## Shot grammar
A shot is a framing of the board in one state of accumulation. Between shots the
board gains an item, so cutting back wider reveals what arrived. Every shot
carries continuous slow motion: a 4 to 32 percent zoom plus a lateral drift of
up to 6 percent of frame, with the direction reversed on each cut so the film
never settles. Items sit at 1 to 3 degrees of rotation, held by tape or a clip.
Red thread arrives late, at the ruling, and connects the notice to the opinion.

## Rhythm spec
About 38 shots per minute, the slowest in the set. Maximum static hold 3
seconds; measured 2.00. There is never a frozen frame, even in a hold. Pauses
go on the two beats where an item lands: the surplus label and the vote stamp.

## Asset recipe
100 percent composite: the board is built in HTML from 3 document facsimiles and
3 generated stills, then screenshotted at nine states of accumulation. No shot
is a bare stock clip. Generated imagery is only ever a photograph pinned to the
board, never a full frame.

## Cost per 10 minute episode
Generation calls: about 12 stills, since each one becomes a pinned photograph
and can be reused across states. Stock clips: 0. Board build: about 5 hours for
the first episode, then 1 hour per episode to restate. Editing: about 5 hours.
Cheap after the first build, and the most reusable style in the set.

## Three rules that must never be broken
1. Never freeze. If a shot has no drift it has failed, no matter how long it is.
2. One emerald item per board. The surplus is the only thing that gets it.
3. Nothing is straight and nothing is centred. The moment the board looks
   designed rather than assembled, the style dies.

## What this style is bad at
Speed and clarity of a single number. It is a slow accumulating look, so it
cannot land a fast statistic, and a viewer on a phone will struggle to read any
document pinned to the board. Treat pinned documents as texture, and put any
figure the viewer must read on a label.
