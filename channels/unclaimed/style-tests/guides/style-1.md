# Style 1 · Redacted Document

## Look in one sentence
A real document fills the frame in near darkness, and exactly one line burns
emerald while everything around it stays dim.

## Palette
| Hex | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `#050505` | Ground. The paper is inverted, so the page body reads as near black. |
| `#3AAE5C` | The lit line. Emerald is reserved for the single fact currently landing, and for money. Nothing else is ever this colour. |
| `#F5F0E3` | Second sign-off line only. Bone white closes the film. |
| `#8A8578` | Caption grey under a figure. Says what the figure is. |

Dim layer sits between 34 and 80 percent of the inverted page depending on the
shot, so consecutive pages differ in tonal mass. Never let two adjacent shots
sit at the same dim level.

## Typography
The document supplies its own type: Times New Roman for the court filing,
Arial for the county listing, Courier for the notice. Do not add display type
except for a figure. Figures are Arial Black, 112 px on a 720 px frame (roughly
one sixth of frame height), centred in the lower third on an opaque plate at
`#050505` so they never sit on the lit line. The caption under a figure is
Arial Bold, 22 px, letter spaced 0.26 em, upper case.

## Shot grammar
Three framings only, and they alternate:
- **Wide**: the page fills the frame, one line lit.
- **Mid**: a block of the page, one line lit.
- **Close**: the lit line fills the frame, so emerald dominates.

Each shot is a slow push of 4 to 5 percent. Cuts are hard, with no transition.
The rule that carries the style: **never two lit lines at once, and never two
consecutive shots from the same document.** Cut wide page, close line, new
document, and repeat.

## Rhythm spec
About 48 shots per minute. Maximum static hold 2.5 seconds; measured 2.47.
The only pause is the sign-off, which gets three beats over the last 3.5
seconds so it still moves while the voice lands.

## Asset recipe
100 percent document facsimile, screenshotted from hand built HTML at 2x.
Zero stock, zero generated imagery, zero screen recording. Type is rendered as
transparent PNG. This is the cheapest style in the set by a wide margin.

## Cost per 10 minute episode
Generation calls: 0. Stock clips: 0. Documents: 6 to 10 HTML facsimiles,
about 3 hours to build and tag. Editing: about 4 hours, most of it choosing
which line lights on which word. Total about 7 hours, no spend.

## Three rules that must never be broken
1. One lit line at a time. Two is an instant fail.
2. Emerald is only ever the lit fact or money. Never decoration.
3. Every figure carries a grey caption saying what it is. A number alone on a
   document invites the viewer to misread which number it is.

## What this style is bad at
Anything physical. It cannot show a house, a person, a place or a feeling. It
has no warmth and no faces, so a 10 minute episode in this style alone will
feel airless. Use it for the evidence spine and cut away to something with air.
