# Style 8 · Data First

**Matrix:** vector data graphics / paper white / chart / medium build.

## Look in one sentence
The chart is the protagonist: a bar climbs, a dot field resolves, and the type
stays small enough that the shape carries it.

## Palette
| Hex | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `#F7F5F0` | Paper. Warm white, never pure white, so ink reads as ink. |
| `#14161A` | Ink. Bars, dots, rules and type. |
| `#D5D2CA` | The unmarked dot. Present but quiet. |
| `#3AAE5C` | The marked dot and one accent rule. Emerald means the thing being counted. |
| `#6B6F76` | Mono kicker and caption grey. |

## Typography
Public Sans only, and small. Kickers 13 px mono at 0.20 em, captions 15 px,
figures 74 to 150 px. A figure is the only large thing, and never sits inside
the chart it describes.

## Shot grammar
One chart per shot, and every shot reframes as well as relaying out. A bar
climbs by a paper coloured box sliding up off it, and the figure locks only once
the bar has arrived. A dot field is seven across so the ratio is countable. No
scale ticks, ever: a tick value is a figure the script did not state.

## Rhythm spec
About 37 shots per minute. Maximum static hold 2.0 s; measured 1.97. The build
is medium: each chart needs a beat to be read before it is cut.

## Asset recipe
100 percent hand built HTML and SVG. Zero stock, zero generation, zero footage.

## Cost per 10 minute episode
Generation calls: 0. About 5 hours to build the chart system, 20 minutes per
chart, 5 hours edit.

## Three rules that must never be broken
1. No intermediate figure. The bar may climb; the number resolves from
   redaction and never counts up.
2. No axis values, no ticks, no gridlines carrying numbers.
3. Emerald marks the counted thing and nothing else.

## What this style is bad at
Everything a chart cannot say. It has no place, no object, no person and no
texture, so it cannot open an episode or carry a stake. On a paper white ground
it is also the hardest style in the set to cut: two sparse layouts in a row do
not register as a cut, so composition has to change, not just content.
