## Positioning

Unclaimed reveals money, benefits, compensation, refunds, settlements and
financial rights that ordinary people do not know exist. Every episode
opens on the same possibility: there may be money connected to your name,
your situation, or something that happened to you.

Not a personal injury channel. Coverage: unclaimed property, insurance
benefits, class action settlements, employment compensation, consumer
refunds, government programmes, tax credits, banking errors, travel
compensation, property deposits, estates and inheritance, data breach
settlements, product recalls, medical billing refunds.

## Editorial boundaries

**Does:** explain what the money is, why it exists, who may qualify, where
it is held, what the process looks like, what proof is usually needed, what
mistakes prevent recovery, where official information can be checked.

**Does not:** guarantee eligibility or payment, promise a settlement value,
imply every viewer has money waiting, use manufactured urgency, present
general information as personalised legal/tax/financial advice, or turn an
episode into a lead form.

**The funnel stays separate from the educational answer.** Any commercial
pathway (lead gen, affiliate) is visibly separate from the answer itself.
This rule protects the channel's entire value.

## Production notes specific to this channel

- Topic selection from verified consumer search demand, not interest.
- Research official sources first: state treasury sites, settlement
  administrator pages, court dockets, agency refund programmes.
- Build the eligibility/process map before writing a word — the map is the
  episode; the script narrates it.
- Hard gate: **no claim ships without a live official URL in the script
  file.** Human fact-check sign-off is mandatory.
- Screen-record official portals and cut them in directly. Never recreate
  a government portal as an illustration.
- One long-form plus three Shorts, cut from the "how to check" beat.
- Mine comments for the next high-intent question — the comment section is
  this channel's topic pipeline.

## Monetisation lanes (kept separate from the answer)

AdSense · lead generation (personal injury, workers comp, mass tort
screening, insurance/consumer legal enquiries, firm matching) · affiliate
(identity/credit monitoring, tax prep, estate services, flight
compensation, subscription cancellation, insurance comparison, data
removal) · sponsorship · digital checklists · newsletter **The Unclaimed
Report** (weekly: new settlements, deadlines, refund programmes, recalls,
benefits, official databases).
